# Business analysis
1. Restate the decision and the goal in one line. Name what is unknown.
2. List 2 to 4 real options, including "do nothing". For each: cost (money, hours), main benefit, main risk, how reversible it is.
3. Use the owner's own facts from the vault (projects, hardware, time) before generic advice; label them VAULT. Label guesses ASSUMED.
4. Recommend ONE option, say what would change your mind, and give the smallest first step that tests it.
5. Keep numbers honest: show the arithmetic, mark estimates, never invent market data; fetch it with web_search and cite it.
