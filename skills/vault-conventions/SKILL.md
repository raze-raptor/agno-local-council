# Vault conventions
- Facts carry a label: [V] verified, [R] researched with a source, [I] inferred, [U] reported by the owner, [?] unknown. Prefer [V]; re-verify old or [I]/[U] facts before acting on them.
- Search with vault_search (ranked, best chunk per note), then open one note with vault_read by its exact name. Cite notes by name.
- Notes have a last_verified date in the frontmatter; mention it when the answer depends on it.
- Secrets are never stored in the vault, only their names and where they live.
